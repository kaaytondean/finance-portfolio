What the map says

The fight is for the primary account

Chime and SoFi both grow by becoming where your paycheck lands. Chime added 1.5M active members in 2025, and about 40% of SoFi's product growth came from existing members.

BNPL is turning into banking

Klarna's banking customers doubled to 15.8M, and Affirm's card is pulling pay later into everyday spending. The segment lines on this map are starting to blur.

Recurring revenue beats trading cycles

Coinbase's subscription revenue grew while trading revenue barely moved. Robinhood Gold reached 4.2M subscribers. Investors reward revenue that doesn't depend on market swings.
